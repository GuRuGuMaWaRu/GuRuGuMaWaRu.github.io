A few words about me

I took up coding back in 2013 and was astounded! I remember going through all courses on Treehouse — everything looked so delicious and fun!

And then came freeCodeCamp and that was an entirely different level. Their course projects were the real deal — instead of writing code after tutors you were given User Stories and general requirements. Then it was up to you to implement the solution. This freedom was scary but also exciting!
